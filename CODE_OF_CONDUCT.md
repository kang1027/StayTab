# Code of Conduct

StayTab welcomes people who want to improve the project, report a problem, ask a thoughtful question, or help another user.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, respectful, and specific.
- Critique behavior, code, and ideas rather than people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence when it matters.
- Respect privacy. Remove personal information from logs, screenshots, and recordings.
- Keep issue and review threads focused on the project and the reported workflow.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, sexualized content, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community. Serious or privacy-sensitive conduct reports should be sent through a private GitHub security advisory rather than a public issue.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, repetition, and whether the participant makes a good-faith effort to repair the harm.
